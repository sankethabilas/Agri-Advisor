## Document ID Convention

IDs follow this format:

`<CROP>-<CATEGORY>-<NUMBER>`

Examples:

- `R-D-001` → Rice – Disease – 001
- `R-T-001` → Rice – Treatment – 001
- `R-C-001` → Rice – Cultivation – 001
- `T-D-001` → Tomato – Disease – 001
- `M-D-001` → Maize – Disease – 001
